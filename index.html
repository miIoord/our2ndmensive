a letter from drew

sayang,

i've been thinking about what i wanted to put here for a while. there are so many things i could say about these two months, but i don't want this to just be another message about how much i miss you. there's something bigger than that that i want you to understand.

i really did love you.

i loved you in the way that made me want to try things i had never tried before. i learned things because of you. i became curious about things i probably would've ignored if i had never met you. you made me want to understand more, do more, and become someone who could give you more.

and i think you saw that in the little things i did. especially in the things i made for you.

like this website.

i stayed up late making this because i wanted to give you something that wasn't just another message i could type and send. i wanted something that had my time in it. my effort. my thoughts. something that i could actually build with my own hands and give to you.

there were nights when i was already tired and still kept working on it. fixing things, changing things, trying again when something didn't work. and honestly, i didn't mind.

because it was for you.

that's how i've always tried to love you. not just by saying that i care, but by actually doing something with the love i had.

i tried to make things for you with my own effort. i tried to find ways to make you happy. i tried to understand the things you liked. i tried to learn things i didn't know. i tried to give you the best version of what i could give.

and sometimes, i cared about you more than i cared about myself.

i would rather know that you were okay than worry about whether i was okay. i would rather carry something myself if it meant making things a little easier for you. i'm not saying that to make you feel guilty. i just want you to know that when i loved you, i really meant it.

i gave you a part of myself that i don't give easily.

so yes, everything that happened between us hurts. of course it does. because what we had wasn't something small to me.

we built something.

we had our own little world, our own conversations, our own memories, our own way of understanding each other. there were things that only made sense because it was us.

and i don't want all of that to simply disappear.

i don't want everything we've built to fall apart just because we reached a difficult part of our story. i don't want the time, effort, memories, and feelings we put into this to suddenly become something that only belongs in the past.

because i still believe in what we had.

and more importantly, i still believe in what we can have.

i know we can't erase what happened. i know there are things we need to understand, things we need to learn from, and things we could've handled differently.

but i don't think mistakes automatically mean something has to be over.

sometimes i think they can be the reason we learn how to do things better.

that's what i want to believe about us.

i still want this relationship to exist. i still want there to be an us. not because i'm trying to force you into something, but because i genuinely believe there's still something between us worth fighting for.

i still believe we have a road ahead of us. maybe we can't see the whole thing yet, but i believe it's there.

and somewhere in me, i still believe you can feel that too. i still believe there's a part of you that doesn't want everything we've built to just disappear.

i can't speak for you, and i won't pretend that i know everything you're feeling. but i hope you haven't completely let go of the thought of us either.

because i haven't.

if there's still a way for us to find each other again, i want to believe we'll find it. not by pretending nothing happened, but by understanding what happened and choosing to do better after it.

i don't want to throw away something that meant this much to both of us just because we didn't know how to hold it perfectly the first time.

you're still someone i love. you're still someone i care about. you're still someone who made me brave enough to learn things i never thought i'd learn.

and you're still someone i want to have more memories with.

so when you look at this website, i hope you don't only see a two-month gift.

i hope you see the hours behind it. the effort. the things i learned while making it. the nights i stayed awake because i wanted it to be something you'd like.

i hope you see me in it.

because that's what these two months are to me. they're not just a number or a date. they're proof that we happened. proof that we cared. proof that we built something together.

and i still believe something built with that much love doesn't have to end by simply falling apart.

happy two months, sayang.

i don't know exactly what comes next, but i know what i still hope for.

i still hope for you. i still hope for us. and i still believe our story has more pages left.

still yours,
drew
