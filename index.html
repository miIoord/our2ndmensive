a letter from drew

sayang,

i've been thinking about what i wanted to write here for a while. i had so many things in my head, and honestly, i still don't know how to put all of them into words without making this way too long. but i wanted to try anyway.

first of all, happy two months.

i know things between us aren't exactly how they were when we first started, but i still wanted to be here for this day. because no matter what happened, these two months were real to me. what we had was real to me.

and i hope you know how much i really did love you.

i loved you enough to actually try. not just say that i cared, but do something with it. i wanted to make you happy in whatever ways i could, even if sometimes it was just little things that probably didn't look like much from the outside.

i tried to make things for you with my own effort. i tried to understand the things you liked. i tried things i had never done before. i learned things i probably would've never bothered learning if i hadn't met you.

you made me curious about so many things. you made me want to learn, try, build, and figure things out just because i wanted to be able to give something back to you.

and yeah, that includes this website.

i stayed up making this because i didn't want to just send you another message and call it a day. i wanted to actually make something for you. something that had my time in it, my effort, and a little bit of everything i've been feeling.

there were times when i was already tired and still kept working on it, fixing things that broke, changing things i didn't like, learning something new just so i could make one part work.

and i didn't really mind doing any of it.

because it was for you.

i think that's one of the clearest ways i know how to love someone. i don't always know how to say everything perfectly, but when i care about someone, i try to show it.

sometimes i probably cared about you more than i knew how to take care of myself. i'd worry about whether you were okay before thinking about whether i was okay. i'd rather try to make things easier for you than complain about how difficult they were for me.

i'm not saying any of that so you feel like you owe me something. you don't. i just want you to know that when i loved you, i really meant it.

that's why losing the way we used to be hurt as much as it did. not because i think everything between us was perfect, but because it mattered to me.

we had our own little things. our own conversations, jokes, memories, habits, and all those small moments that probably wouldn't mean the same thing to anyone else.

i don't want to pretend those things never happened. i don't want to look at everything we built and act like it suddenly stopped meaning something just because we reached a difficult point.

and after reading what you said to me, i think i understand something a little better now.

i don't want us to jump back into something just because we miss each other and then end up hurting each other again. i don't want us to pretend everything is okay when there are still things we need to understand.

if taking things slowly is what we need right now, then i'm okay with that.

i can stay.

i can give us the time to figure things out properly. to understand each other better, to learn from what happened, and to see what we can do differently this time.

i don't need us to have every answer right now. i don't need to know exactly what we're going to become.

i just don't want to close the door on us.

because hearing you say that you still care, that you've missed us too, and that you still want to see if there's a healthy way forward... honestly, that means more to me than you probably realize.

i won't pretend i know exactly what you're feeling. i don't want to put words in your mouth or assume that we're already going somewhere.

but i do hope we can keep choosing to stay. even if it's slowly. even if it's just one conversation at a time. even if some days are harder than others.

i still care about you. i still love you. and i still want to know you, not just as the person i used to call mine, but as you are now.

i want to understand the parts of you i didn't understand before. i want you to understand me too. and if we're going to find our way back to each other, i want it to be because we actually learned how to treat each other better, not because we were afraid of letting go.

i also don't want you to look at everything i made for you and think it was all just me trying to get something from you.

i made these things because i wanted to. because having you in my life made me want to create things, learn things, and put effort into something that mattered to me.

this website is just one of those things.

every little part of it took time. i had to figure things out, mess things up, fix them, and learn things i didn't know before. and honestly, i'm glad i did.

because now when i look at it, i don't just see a website. i see something i made because you mattered enough for me to want to make it.

and that's what these two months mean to me too. not just a date. not just a number. but a part of my life that i'm genuinely grateful happened.

whatever happens from here, i don't want to erase that.

i don't want to erase you.

and i don't want to rush us either.

so let's take it slowly. let's stay. let's talk when we can, understand each other better, and see where this takes us.

i don't know what the next part of our story looks like yet. but for the first time in a while, i don't feel like i need to know right away.

i'm just glad you still care. i'm glad you still miss us. and i'm glad there's still a little space for us to figure things out instead of simply walking away from everything.

happy two months, sayang.

thank you for these two months. thank you for everything you gave me, everything you taught me, and for being someone who made me want to become better at things i never thought i'd even try.

i'll still be here. slowly, without rushing you.

and if there's still a way for us, i'd like to find it with you.

yours,
drew
